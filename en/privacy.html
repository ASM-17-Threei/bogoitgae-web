Privacy Policy

Bogoitgae (the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws and does its best to keep users' personal information safe. This Policy explains how the personal information of users of the Company's dog monitoring mobile application, Bogoitgae (the "App"), is collected, used, stored and destroyed.

Effective date: September 22, 2026

한국어

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

Article 1 (Personal Information Collected and How It Is Collected)

The Company collects only the minimum personal information needed to provide the App.

1. Collected through social account sign-in

When a user signs in with a social account (Kakao, Google or Apple), the Company receives the following information from that provider and stores it.

Item collectedPurpose
Member number (unique social login identifier)Member identification and sign-in
Nickname (profile name)Displaying the user in the App

The same items apply to Kakao, Google and Apple.

※ The Company does not directly collect or store users' email addresses, passwords, phone numbers or real names.

2. Information users enter while using the App

CategoryItems collected
Dog informationName, breed, date of birth, sex, neutered status, weight, separation anxiety status, health conditions
Device (camera) informationCamera pairing identifier, model name, OS version, app version
Notification settingsPush notification preference, report delivery time and days, device push token (FCM)
Video and imagesRecorded event video clips, thumbnails, dog profile photos
Report detailsReason and details of false detection reports for events

3. Information generated and collected automatically

  • App usage records: last access time, camera connection and disconnection history
  • Operational metrics: performance statistics such as server request processing time (no personally identifying information)
  • App usage statistics: screen visit history, app launch and exit times, app version, device type and operating system, a device identifier generated by the App, and session replay (a recording that replays the App's screen layout and interaction flow)
    The user's use of the screens is recorded each time the App is used.
    Camera video areas (live view, event video clips, camera preview) are masked and are not included in the recording.

※ The Company does not separately store users' IP addresses. The IP address collection feature is also turned off when collecting app usage statistics.

Article 2 (Purposes of Processing Personal Information)

① Member identification, sign-in and provision of the App
② Dog monitoring, and recording, storing and playing event video
③ Sending alerts about unusual situations (push notifications) and reports
④ Identifying events through video analysis and improving App quality
⑤ Responding to inquiries, handling disputes and preventing misuse of the App

Article 3 (Processing and Retention Periods)

CategoryRetention period
Member information (social login identifier, dog and device information, etc.)Until the member deletes their account
Event video clips and thumbnailsAutomatically destroyed 24 hours after recording
Video clips reported as false detectionsSee Article 7 (kept separately for quality improvement)
Records of reasons for account deletionFor the period set by applicable law, to prevent misuse

Where retention is required by applicable law, the information is kept for the period set by that law.

Article 4 (Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties, except where the user has given prior consent or where required by law.

Article 5 (Entrustment of Personal Information Processing)

The Company entrusts the following personal information processing tasks in order to operate the App smoothly.

Entrusted partyEntrusted task
Amazon Web Services (AWS)Cloud storage and management of video, images and data (Seoul region)
Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)Sending mobile push notifications
Amplitude, Inc.Collecting and analyzing app and website usage statistics

Article 6 (Transfer of Personal Information Overseas)

The Company uses an overseas artificial intelligence (AI) analysis service to analyze video and identify events, and video and images are transferred overseas for this purpose.

ItemDetails
RecipientOpenRouter, Inc. (https://openrouter.ai)
Destination countryUnited States
Time and method of transferSent over an information and communications network when video analysis is needed
Items transferredEvent video clips and images
Purpose of useAnalyzing situations in video and identifying events
Retention and use periodUntil analysis processing is complete

The Company may perform part of the video analysis on infrastructure in Korea, but the overseas transfer described above occurs when the overseas AI analysis service is used. Users who do not want this overseas transfer may stop using the related features.

The Company also uses an overseas analytics service to analyze app usage statistics, and the following information is transferred overseas for this purpose.

ItemDetails
RecipientAmplitude, Inc. (https://amplitude.com)
Destination countryUnited States
Time and method of transferSent over an information and communications network when the relevant event occurs during app use
Items transferredScreen visit history, feature usage records (camera connection, viewing live view, video and reports, sending and opening notifications, false detection reports), the dog's age group and size category, app launch and exit times, app version, device type and operating system, a device identifier generated by the App, member number, and recordings of screen use (session replay)
Purpose of useAnalyzing app usage statistics and improving the service
Retention and use periodOne year from the date of collection

Areas showing video and images recorded by the camera (live view, event video clips, camera preview) are masked and are not included in these items.
Therefore, video of the home and of the dog is not sent to Amplitude.
Information that directly identifies users, such as names and contact details, is also not included.

The same Amplitude service is used for visit statistics on the website (bogoitgae.com). The items transferred in that case are the pages visited, the referral source (ad link information and the previous site address), clicks on the app download button, browser type and an identifier generated by the browser (a random value). The destination country and retention period are the same as above.

Article 7 (Separate Retention and Pseudonymization of Video Reported as False Detections)

If a user reports an event alert as a false detection, the video clip concerned is excluded from the 24-hour automatic destruction and may be kept to improve App accuracy (AI retraining).

① The Company may pseudonymize such video under Article 28-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and use it for statistics, scientific research and App quality improvement.
② Original video that has not been pseudonymized is not viewed or used for anything other than the above purposes.
③ Users may request deletion of such video at any time.

Article 8 (Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them)

Users may exercise the following rights at any time.

① Request access to personal information
② Request correction of errors
③ Request deletion
④ Request suspension of processing
⑤ Delete their account (withdraw consent)

Rights may be exercised through features in the App or through the contact details of the Privacy Officer below, and the Company will act without delay.

Article 9 (Automated Decisions)

The Company analyzes recorded video using artificial intelligence (AI) to automatically identify events such as unusual situations, and provides alerts and reports based on the results.

① If this processing is an automated decision that significantly affects a user's rights or obligations under Article 37-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, the user may refuse the decision or request an explanation of it.
② AI analysis results are not guaranteed to be fully accurate, and the App is a tool that assists in checking on a dog's condition.
③ Related requests may be made through the contact details of the Privacy Officer below.

Article 10 (Procedure and Method of Destroying Personal Information)

① Personal information whose retention period has expired or whose processing purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay.
② Event video clips and thumbnails are automatically destroyed 24 hours after recording.
③ When a member deletes their account, member information and related video are deleted.
④ Electronic files are permanently deleted in a way that cannot be recovered or played back, and the originals in cloud storage are also deleted.

Article 11 (Measures to Keep Personal Information Secure)

① Administrative measures: minimizing the number of staff who handle personal information, managing access rights, and training those staff
② Technical measures: access control and permission management for personal information and video storage, and encryption in transit
③ Preventing misuse of access to personal information and minimizing the handling of personal information

Article 12 (Personal Information of Children Under 14)

The Company does not collect personal information from children under the age of 14. The App is intended for users aged 14 or older.

Article 13 (Automatic Collection Tools and Advertising Identifiers)

① The App does not use cookies. However, for app usage statistics it uses a device identifier generated by the App (a random value), which links usage records from the same device. This identifier is not the device's unique number and is removed when the App is deleted.
For visit statistics, the website keeps an identifier generated by the browser (a random value) in browser storage (cookies, etc.). It is removed when browsing history and cookies are cleared, and storage can be refused in the browser settings.
② The Company does not collect advertising identifiers (ADID/IDFA) for advertising purposes.
③ The Company configures app and website usage statistics so that IP addresses and location information are not collected.
